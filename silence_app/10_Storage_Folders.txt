Supabase Storage Structure – SILENCE App

Bucket: silence_assets (public bucket – read by anyone, write restricted)
├── library_photos/
│   └── {library_id}/
│       ├── cover_1.jpg
│       ├── cover_2.jpg
│       ├── interior_1.jpg
│       └── ...
├── qr_assets/
│   └── {library_id}/
│       ├── joining_qr_{version}.png
│       └── attendance_qr_{version}.png
└── default_avatars/
    ├── admin_default.png
    └── member_default.png

Bucket: silence_private (private bucket – only owner or uploader can access)
├── member_profiles/
│   └── {member_id}/
│       ├── profile_photo.jpg
│       ├── id_proof_aadhaar.jpg
│       └── id_proof_pan.jpg
├── payment_proofs/
│   └── {payment_id}/
│       ├── upi_screenshot.png
│       └── cash_receipt.jpg
├── expense_receipts/
│   └── {expense_id}/
│       └── receipt.jpg
└── admin_documents/
    └── {admin_id}/
        └── id_proof.jpg

Bucket: silence_temp (public but auto-delete after 24 hours)
└── temp_uploads/
    └── {session_id}/
        └── *.tmp

RLS / Storage Policies (Supabase)

Bucket: silence_assets
- SELECT: authenticated users or anon (if library is active)
- INSERT: authenticated (admin only for library_photos; system for QR assets)
- UPDATE: authenticated (admin only for their own library_photos)
- DELETE: authenticated (admin only for their own library_photos)

Folder-specific:
  library_photos/{library_id}/
    - SELECT: anyone (public library listing)
    - INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: admin with matching library_id
  qr_assets/{library_id}/
    - SELECT: admin with matching library_id; members can view their library's QR
    - INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: admin with matching library_id
  default_avatars/
    - SELECT: anyone
    - INSERT: system only (pre-seeded)

Bucket: silence_private
- SELECT: authenticated user who is either:
    - the member (profile_photos, id_proofs)
    - the library owner (for payment_proofs, expense_receipts of their library)
    - admin with specific access
- INSERT: authenticated user uploading their own data
- UPDATE: same as SELECT
- DELETE: same as SELECT (plus system cleanup after retention expiry)

Folder-specific:
  member_profiles/{member_id}/
    - SELECT: member_id = auth.uid() OR admin of member's library
    - INSERT: member_id = auth.uid()
  payment_proofs/{payment_id}/
    - SELECT: admin of the library linked to payment OR member who made payment
    - INSERT: member who made payment
  expense_receipts/{expense_id}/
    - SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: admin of the library linked to expense
  admin_documents/{admin_id}/
    - SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: admin_id = auth.uid()

Bucket: silence_temp
- SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: authenticated user (temporary, any operation)
- Auto-delete policy: TTL 24 hours via Supabase Storage lifecycle rule.

Naming Conventions

- library_id, member_id, payment_id, expense_id → UUID format (no spaces)
- All image files: max 5 MB, JPG or PNG
- ID proof files: max 10 MB (PDF allowed for Aadhaar)
- QR assets: PNG, 500x500px

Retention Rules

- Payment proofs: kept for 7 years (legal requirement)
- Member ID proofs: kept for 1 year after membership exit, then auto-deleted
- Library photos: kept as long as library exists
- Temporary uploads: deleted after 24 hours
