# Community & Content Policy — Silence

**Silence – Library Management & QR Attendance**

**Last updated: 28 June 2026**

> Published at **https://silenceapp.in/community** and available in-app. This covers community standards, acceptable content, reporting/abuse, and copyright complaints.

---

## 1. Be respectful

Silence connects students and library owners. Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind are not allowed.

## 2. Content that is NOT allowed

You may not post, upload, or share content that:
- is illegal, fraudulent, or infringes anyone's rights;
- is obscene, sexually explicit, or harmful to minors;
- is hateful, abusive, defamatory, or harassing;
- is spam, misleading, or impersonates another person or business;
- contains malware, or someone else's personal/identity data without consent;
- attempts to manipulate reviews, attendance, or payments.

## 3. User-generated content (reviews, photos, messages, names)

- You are responsible for the content you post (such as library reviews, photos, and messages).
- By posting, you grant Silence a licence to host and display it to operate the Services (see Terms, Section 10).
- We may remove content and suspend or terminate accounts that violate this Policy — with or without notice.

## 4. Reporting and moderation

- Any user can **report** content or a library that violates this Policy using the in-app **Report** option, or by emailing **ashish.premierbro@gmail.com** with details and a screenshot.
- We aim to **review reported content within 24 hours** and will remove content or take action where appropriate.
- Library Owners can remove or hide reviews and content on their own library; Silence can remove any content on the platform.
- Users may request to **block** abusive interactions; contact us if the in-app tools are insufficient.

## 5. Copyright / Intellectual-property complaints

If you believe content on Silence infringes your copyright or other IP rights, email **ashish.premierbro@gmail.com** with:
1. Your name and contact details;
2. Identification of the work that is infringed;
3. The location (screen / link) of the infringing content in the app;
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is unauthorised;
5. A statement that the information is accurate and you are the rights holder or authorised to act.

We will review and, where justified, remove the content and may suspend the responsible account. Repeat infringers will be terminated.

## 6. Enforcement

Violations may lead to content removal, warnings, suspension, or permanent termination, at our discretion, and may be reported to authorities where required by law.

## 7. Changes

We may update this Policy with a revised "Last updated" date.
