Silence is a **neutral technology platform**. It provides software that **library and study-space owners ("Library Owners")** use to manage their spaces, seats, shifts, attendance and members ("**Members**" / "students"). Silence does **not** run libraries, does **not** collect fees from Members, and is **not** a party to the relationship between a Library Owner and a Member.

## 2. Our role under India's data-protection law (DPDP Act, 2023)

Because Silence is a neutral platform, responsibility for personal data is split:

- For a **Member's** personal data that a **Library Owner** chooses to collect (for example, ID documents, contact details, attendance), the **Library Owner is the Data Fiduciary** (they decide why the data is collected) and **Silence acts as a Data Processor** (we only store and process it on the Library Owner's instructions and on their behalf).
- For **account, authentication, app-usage and Library-Owner subscription data** that **we** decide the purpose of, **Silence is the Data Fiduciary**.

Members should also review the privacy practices of the specific Library they join. Silence is not responsible for a Library Owner's independent data practices.

## 3. Information we collect

**a) Information you provide when creating an account (Members and Library Owners):**
- Name (and optional nickname), email address, mobile number
- Password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text)
- For Members: date of birth, gender, address, and a profile photo
- For Library Owners: library name, address, photos, opening hours, payment handles (e.g., UPI IDs) used to receive payments from their own Members

**b) Identity / verification documents (optional, collected by Library Owners):**
- A Library Owner may ask a Member to upload identity or verification document images. **Silence does not require, request, or use these documents for its own purposes** — they are collected by, and belong to, the Library Owner.
- These documents are stored in **restricted, owner-scoped private storage** and are not publicly accessible.

**c) Usage and attendance data:**
- QR check-in / check-out records, session times, seat and shift information, membership status, and payment records that a Library Owner records inside the app.

**d) Device and technical data:**
- App version, device model, operating system, crash and diagnostic logs, and a push-notification token (to deliver notifications).

**e) Location data:**
- With your permission, your device's approximate/precise location is used **only while the app is in use** to help you **find nearby libraries**. We do **not** track location in the background.

We do **not** knowingly collect data from anyone under **18 years of age** (see Section 11).

## 4. How we use your information

We use information to:
- Create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in (including Google and Apple sign-in);
- Provide the core features: QR attendance, seat/shift management, memberships, notifications, and (for Members) finding nearby libraries;
- Allow Library Owners to manage their own Members and records;
- Send transactional and service notifications (e.g., approvals, reminders);
- Maintain security, prevent abuse and fraud, and debug crashes;
- Comply with law and respond to lawful requests and grievances.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** use your data for third-party advertising.

## 5. Legal basis / consent

We process personal data based on your **consent** (given when you create an account and accept this Policy and our Terms), to **perform the service** you requested, and to meet **legal obligations**. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (Section 9); withdrawing consent may mean you can no longer use the service.

## 6. Payments

- **Member → Library payments are made outside Silence** (for example, via a UPI app). **Silence never receives, holds, processes, or stores your card/bank/UPI credentials or the payment itself.** We only record that a Library Owner marked a payment as received.
- **Library-Owner subscriptions to Silence are free during the current beta.** If paid plans are introduced later, payments will be handled by a third-party payment provider (e.g., **Razorpay**) under their own privacy policy; Silence will not store your full card/bank details.

## 8. International data transfer

Our infrastructure provider stores data in the **Seoul (South Korea) region**. By using Silence you acknowledge that your data is processed **outside India**. We take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled securely and in line with this Policy. South Korea is not a country restricted for such transfers under current Indian law.

## 9. Data retention and account deletion

- We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.
- You can request deletion of your account from **inside the app** (Profile → account deletion) or via **https://silenceapp.in/delete-account**.
- When you request deletion, your account is frozen immediately and **permanently deleted within 30 days** (a short recovery window applies, as described in the app).
- Some records may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, tax, security, or dispute-resolution purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.
- Member records held on behalf of a Library Owner are also subject to that Library Owner's own retention decisions.

## 10. Cookies (website only)

Our **website** (silenceapp.in) may use strictly-necessary and basic analytics cookies to keep the site working and understand traffic. The **mobile app does not use advertising cookies.** You can control cookies through your browser settings. Details are provided on the website's cookie notice.

## 11. Children

Silence is intended for users **18 years and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a Library Owner chooses to enrol a Member under 18, the **Library Owner is responsible** for obtaining any consent required by law. If we learn that we hold a child's data without a lawful basis, we will delete it.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including:
- Encryption **in transit** (HTTPS/TLS) and **at rest** (provider-side);
- **Row-level access controls** so users can only access data they are permitted to;
- **Private, owner-scoped storage** for identity/verification documents;
- Access limited on a need-to-know basis.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will act promptly and, where the law requires, notify the **Data Protection Board of India** and affected users.

## 13. Your rights

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **Correct** inaccurate data (most data is editable in-app);
- **Erase** your data by deleting your account;
- **Withdraw consent**;

To exercise these rights, edit your profile in-app or contact us at the email above. For data a Library Owner controls, we may direct your request to that Library Owner.
