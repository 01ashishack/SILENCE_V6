## About Silence

**Silence** is a simple, honest library and study-space management app built for India. It helps **library owners** run their study spaces — seats, shifts, memberships, payments tracking, and **QR-based attendance** — and helps **students** find libraries, check in with a QR code, and keep track of their study time and membership.

Silence is built for the way study libraries actually work in India's growing towns and cities: easy to set up, works on a normal phone, and keeps owners and students on the same page.

Silence is operated by **Ashish Kumar**, a sole proprietor based in **Alwar, Rajasthan, India**, trading as "Silence – Library Management & QR Attendance".

Silence is a **neutral technology platform** — we provide the software. Libraries are owned and run by independent Library Owners; we are not a party to the relationship between a library and its students.

## What we believe

- **Honest software:** we never show fake success. If something fails, we say so.
- **Privacy first:** we collect only what's needed and protect it. See our Privacy Policy.
- **Fair and simple:** clear rules for owners and students alike.
